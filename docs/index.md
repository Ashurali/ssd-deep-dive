<h2 class="sdd-h2">Start here</h2>

This book is for engineers and students who want to understand solid-state drives from the physics of a flash cell up to the protocols, firmware and fleets built on it. It assumes no storage background: [Chapter 0](core/ch0-foundations.md) supplies the computing basics the rest relies on, and readers who know them can start at [Chapter 1](core/ch1-overview.md). Three paths through it:

- **New to storage:** Chapter 0, then Chapters 1–7 in order, with Supplement A after Chapter 4.
- **Firmware engineer:** Chapters 2–4, then Supplements [A](supplements/a-ecc-coding-theory.md) (error correction), [G](supplements/g-firmware.md) (firmware) and [F](supplements/f-security.md) (security); Chapter 7 shows how that code is tested.
- **Validation engineer:** Chapters 1 and 5–7, then Supplements [H](supplements/h-host-stack.md) (host tools), [D](supplements/d-power-management.md) (power) and [I](supplements/i-data-center.md) (data-center drives).

Signposts: ⭐ marks an important section and ⭐⭐ a core one, and 🎬 marks an interactive animation embedded in the page. Every page ends with key takeaways, a bilingual vocabulary table, and questions with hidden answers.
